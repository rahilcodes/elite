Our team

Design is not just seen. It is experienced.

Meet the architects and engineer behind Elite Architecture. Whoever you speak to, you are speaking to someone who knows your project.

Vickram Paul

CEO & Principal Architect

Vickram founded and leads Elite Architecture, and sets the design direction for every project. He works by the studio motto — Vision Evolved. Spaces Revolved. — and is known by clients for being dependable, easy to talk to and hands-on, including regular visits to projects under construction.

Raza Jan

Architect

Raza designs spaces that balance how a building looks with how it works. His eye for detail and his creative approach shape layouts that are practical to build and a pleasure to use.

Johanan Dolphin

Civil Engineer — Consultant

Johanan looks after the structure behind the design. He plans and checks each project for safety, efficiency and long life, so that what is drawn can be built soundly.
